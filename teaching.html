Teaching

In my courses, fundamentals come first — and second, and third. Nature speaks in mathematics, and every engineered system answers in the same tongue; an engineer who has not mastered that language can only guess at what the world is saying. My aim is to give students that fluency: to sharpen their mathematical eye until equations stop being symbols on a page and become a way of seeing.
