Research

! Numerical methods that let a flow and the bodies inside it evolve together — and that show where, and why, a steady flow starts to break down.

Themes

01

Fluid–Structure Interaction

! Rigid and elastic bodies moving with a viscous flow, solved on one fixed grid. Immersed boundary and one-continuum formulations built on velocity–vorticity and vorticity–stream function variables, extended to full-Eulerian fluid–rigid–elastic systems and two-phase wave–body problems.

  • Full-Eulerian fluid–rigid–elastic interaction — Fluid Dyn. Res. 2023
  • Vorticity-based approach to FSI — Fluid Dyn. Res. 2016
  • One-continuum approach — J. Mechanics 2015
02

Fluid Instability

! Locating where instability begins. Energy gradient theory applied to two-fluid Poiseuille flow and Rayleigh–Taylor instability, and turned into a toolbox that maps the likely onset locus from a computed flow field.

  • Energy gradient function for Rayleigh–Taylor instability — Fluid Dyn. Res. 2018
  • Instability of two-fluid plane Poiseuille flow — J. Mechanics 2014
  • FEGC 1.0 — Software Impacts 2024
03

Numerical Linear Algebra

! Krylov subspace solvers for the large sparse systems behind incompressible flow — preconditioning, storage schemes and high-order compact discretisations of Poisson's equation.

  • Krylov methods in HOC schemes — Ann. Appl. Math. 2025
  • HOC-Krylov 1.0 — Software Impacts 2026
